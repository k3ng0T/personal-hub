ABOUT ME

I'm 16 years old, born in China to a Kazakh family, and now living in Kazakhstan. I recently finished school and this year started university. My interest in programming began around the age of 10 with C#, though it wasn't until I turned 12 that I got seriously into web development — learning HTML, CSS, JS, and PHP on my own. By 14, I had picked up Python and SQL as well.

These days, I focus on building tools for myself — task managers, AI assistants, and small utilities that solve problems I run into daily. I've also gotten into AI-assisted development, using it less to write code I don't understand and more to speed up implementing ideas I already know how to build. Outside of programming, I enjoy creating Minecraft datapacks and experimenting with AI prompting.
